# Synthetic Audit Definitions

## MODEL_UPDATED

MODEL_UPDATED means that one or more fields in a synthetic model record were changed.

## MODEL_DELETED

MODEL_DELETED means that a synthetic model record was marked as deleted.

## FAILED

FAILED means that the requested audit operation did not complete successfully.
